Biography


Xiangjie Sui is an assistant professor at the Faculty of Data Science, City University of Macau. He received the B.E., M.A.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees from Jiangxi University of Finance and Economics, Nanchang, China, in 2018, 2021, and 2024, respectively (Supervisor: Prof. Yuming Fang). He was a visiting student with the Department of Computer Science, City University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong SAR, China, from 2023 to 2024 (Supervisor: Prof. Shiqi Wang). His research interests include visual signal processing and quality assessment, virtual reality (VR), and vision-language model robustness.
