Mitigating Perception Bias: A Training-Free Approach to Enhance LMM for Image Quality Assessment

Baoliang Chen, Siyi Pan, Dongxu Wu, Liang Xie, Xiangjie Sui, Lingyu Zhu, Hanwei Zhu

We expose semantic bias in large multimodal models with controlled degradations, then use a training-free prompting scheme to make image-quality judgments more perceptually reliable.

Diagnosing Corruption-Induced Reliability Failures in Vision-Language Models

Xiangjie Sui, Songyang Li, Hanwei Zhu, Baoliang Chen, Yuming Fang, Xin Sun

We benchmark how visual corruptions alter vision-language model responses and introduce a discriminative robustness alignment metric to expose reliability failures in perceptual tasks.

Perceptual Quality Assessment of 360° Images Based on Generative Scanpath Representation

Xiangjie Sui, Hanwei Zhu, Xuelin Liu, Yuming Fang, Shiqi Wang, Zhou Wang

We model the viewing experience of 360° images as generative scanpath sequences, focusing quality assessment on the locally attended regions that drive perceptual judgments.

2AFC Prompting of Large Multimodal Models for Image Quality Assessment

Hanwei Zhu, Xiangjie Sui, Baoliang Chen, Xuelin Liu, Peilin Chen, Yuming Fang, Shiqi Wang

We adapt the two-alternative forced-choice protocol from vision science to probe and quantify the image-quality assessment capability of large multimodal models.

CVPR 2023

ScanDMM: A Deep Markov Model of Scanpath Prediction for 360° Images

Xiangjie Sui, Yuming Fang, Hanwei Zhu, Shiqi Wang, Zhou Wang

ScanDMM learns the temporal dynamics of visual attention to predict realistic scanpaths in 360° scenes, beyond static saliency maps and unconstrained generative trajectories.
