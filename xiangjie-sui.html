Biography

Xiangjie Sui is an assistant professor at the Faculty of Data Science, City University of Macau. He received his B.E., M.A.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees from Jiangxi University of Finance and Economics in 2018, 2021, and 2024, respectively, supervised by Prof. Yuming Fang. From 2023 to 2024, he was a visiting student at the Department of Computer Science, City University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Shiqi Wang.

His research interests include visual signal processing and quality assessment, virtual reality, and vision-language model robustness.
