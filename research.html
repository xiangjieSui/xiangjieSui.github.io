Research

Research Areas

We connect human visual perception with computational models for images, videos, immersive media, and vision-language systems. Our work asks how visual content is experienced, measured, explored, and made reliable.

01

Visual Quality Assessment

We develop perceptually aligned models that estimate the quality of images and videos as people experience them. This includes no-reference quality assessment, generative scanpath representations, and large multimodal models for image quality assessment.

Perceptual modeling · Image/video quality · LMM-based assessment

Related publications

Perceptual Quality Assessment of 360° Images Based on Generative Scanpath Representation, TIP 2025 2AFC Prompting of Large Multimodal Models for Image Quality Assessment, TCSVT 2024 Perceptual Quality Assessment of Omnidirectional Images as Moving Camera Videos, TVCG 2022

02

Visual Attention

We model not only where observers look, but also the sequence in which they explore a scene. These scanpath models help explain visual attention and support perceptual analysis for 360° images and videos.

Saliency · Scanpath prediction · Generative attention models

Related publications

ScanDMM: A Deep Markov Model of Scanpath Prediction for 360° Images, CVPR 2023 Benchmarking 360° Saliency Models by General-Purpose Metrics, MMSP 2022

03

Vision-Language Model Robustness

We examine how visual corruptions and perception bias affect vision-language models, then develop practical ways to diagnose failures and improve their use in perceptual tasks such as image quality assessment.

Corruption diagnosis · Perception bias · Reliable multimodal models

Related publications

Diagnosing Corruption-Induced Reliability Failures in Vision-Language Models, arXiv 2025
